1. Explorer `vista=tree` and `vista=min` SVGs, plus per-rule figures for automata >30 states — keep the counts and the paginated tables. Saves the figgen/asset work (~0.2–0.3 d) without losing the 4.d.ii evidence, since the NFA→subset graph for P2 remains the demo moment.
2. Rules screen as a separate route — merge its ER/foundation table into the Explorer sidebar (same `GET /api/reglas` payload). Keeps demo priority 5, removes a screen.
3. Email generation in the UI — keep `POST /api/generar` for emails and produce the 10 email pairs from a script; the assignment says "una contraseña **o** correo", so one interactive path is enough.
4. The per-screen `loading` / `offline` states and the `entropia` field — zero demo value, zero grading value.
5. The minimized DFA as a separate explorer view — show the min *count* and the subset graph; the assignment asks Thompson + tree → AFD, not minimization.

## What the plan gets right

- The engine/document correspondence is real, not asserted: I rebuilt the prototype and reproduced the plan's figures exactly — P2 34/9/2, P6 190, P7 158, P8 44, email 136/33/12/8, composition 12 034 tree states. The "9 s build, 70 s with the permutation rule" claim is also accurate (9 s / 59 s measured).
- Per-rule automata plus a per-rule report is the correct architecture for "en qué regla específica falla", and it matches the document's own justification in Annex B.
- Automaton-guided generation is correctly identified as non-negotiable and random strings are explicitly rejected — the right reading of 7.a.iii, and the reason to keep the generator even under time pressure.
- Pre-rendered SVG + `go:embed` is the right call for a Windows demo with no graphviz, and the toolchain assumptions are verified on this machine: Go 1.27.3 and graphviz 16.1 are installed.
- Reusing Annex C for the security level instead of inventing one at app time is correct, including the edge case that a 20-character password containing a blocklisted word is still "Baja" (131 bits at 20 chars is consistent with 6.55 bits/symbol).
